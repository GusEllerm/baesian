Context

The client has to update when an agent files or revises a card. Updates only flow from the server to the browser; your answers go back as ordinary requests.

Recommendation

Server-sent events: one-way is all that's needed, the browser reconnects by itself, and Node's http module serves them without a dependency.

Strongest alternative

WebSockets. One connection could also carry your answers and the presence heartbeat, at the cost of a dependency or a hand-rolled frame parser.

Why now

The live view is being built on it now.

Cost to reverse

Small today: one module. It grows with every view that subscribes to it.

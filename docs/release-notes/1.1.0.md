Baesian 1.1.0 lets several people answer a project's cards: the developers on one repo, say, or a developer and the domain expert whose call some decisions are.

## What's new since 1.0.2

- **A project can have members.** On [baesian.dev](https://baesian.dev), open **Members** beside a project and make a link: it adds one person, once, within seven days, and asks for your passkey. Send it to them yourself; Baesian keeps no address for them. They sign up, open it and join, then run `baesian init` and `baesian login` in their own clone of the repo.
- **Any member can answer any card, and the first answer decides.** A second answer on a card someone has decided isn't recorded, and the desk says who answered first and what they chose.
- **Each card is sent to whoever runs its agent.** It leads their queue, and the others see it after their own, marked with whom it was sent to. A rule's draft, or a proposed change to a setting, is sent to the project's owners.
- **Each person is told of their own cards.** The member a card was sent to is notified as before; the others hear of a blocking or hard card only if it's still waiting half an hour after it arrived. "It's outside my expertise", from the member a card was sent to, tells the others at once.
- **Owners and members.** Owners change the project's settings and rules, add and remove members, and can make someone else an owner. The account that made a project always owns it, and can't leave it. Any other member can leave, or be removed: their machines are signed out at once, their answers on the project's cards stay, their practice answers there are deleted, and the cards sent to them become the owners'.
- **The desk names whoever gave an answer,** saying "you" only when it was you. The decisions by branch say who chose each, and your calibration and practice cards count only the cards you answered.
- **The command line, for members:** `baesian login` in a repo whose project you haven't joined says to ask an owner for a link; `baesian ci` prints every member's passkey line and asks each member to check their own; and `baesian verify` says when a passkey it doesn't pin may be a member's.
- **The privacy policy has changed,** to say what's kept for projects with members, who sees what, and what goes when someone leaves. It's at [baesian.dev/privacy](https://baesian.dev/privacy).
- **Fixed:** the lines of a card's path stayed above their nodes when the desk was scrolled, or when an animation ran late.

A project of one works as it did. No file format changed, and nothing changed in your git hooks or the instructions in `AGENTS.md`. The service still serves the 1.0.0 to 1.0.2 command lines. It adds tables to its accounts database and, as each project wakes, to that project's own database (the object's migration 3), so a fix after it goes forward, not back to 1.0.2.

## Updating

Update the CLI and the plugin together, so the plugin finds its own release on your PATH:

```sh
npm install -g baesian
```

Then the plugin. In Claude Code, from a terminal, then start a new conversation:

```sh
claude plugin update baesian@baesian
```

In Codex, then start Codex's interactive mode and accept its "Hooks need review" prompt:

```sh
codex plugin marketplace upgrade baesian
codex plugin add baesian@baesian
```

Nothing else needs running again. CI pinned to an earlier release by `baesian ci` keeps working; run `baesian ci` again if you want it on 1.1.0, and again whenever someone joins a project whose CI checks decisions, so it pins their passkey. The [setup guide](https://github.com/GusEllerm/baesian/blob/main/docs/setup.md#answering-with-others) has the rest.

# What "Your calibration" shows in a project with members

In a project with several members, should the calibration panel show your own answers, or the project's answers together?

**Decided:** Your own answers, the agent's pick. Then each member's bars count only the cards they answered. What a row needs before its setting can move stays the project's, and says so. A new member's bars start empty.

**Not chosen:** The project's answers together. Then every member sees the same bars, counting everyone's answers, under a heading that says they're the project's. Nobody sees their own record apart from the others'.

**It turned on:** Is the panel for seeing how you yourself answer, or for seeing the evidence the project's settings rest on? (a preference) How you answer: Your own answers. The project's evidence: The project's answers together.

**The fact given:** Today the panel says "Your calibration" and counts every answer in the project, which was the same thing while one person answered. With members it would call another member's answers yours. The gate's settings are one set for the project and move on all members' answers together, whichever is shown. Every member's browser already receives every answer with who gave it, so showing a member their own count reveals nothing new. What an owner sees of another member's record is a later question. The proposal said "The calibration panel is yours"; that wasn't put to you as a card.

**The agent's case:** The panel exists so a reviewer can see whether they're choosing for themselves or following the agent, and that's a fact about one person. Pooled, a member who always agrees is hidden behind one who doesn't, and a new member inherits a record they didn't make. The project's evidence keeps its place beside each row, labelled as the project's.

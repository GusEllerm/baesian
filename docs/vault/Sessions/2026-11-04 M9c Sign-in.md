# 2026-11-04: M9c, accounts and sign-in

A builder agent, in its own worktree off `v1/next`, built the service's side of M9c ([[service]], [[Hosted Service]] section 2, [[File Formats#Accounts (M9c)]]). The website's pages are M9e's and `baesian login` is M9d's.

## Goal

Replace the relay's sign-in seam with real accounts and sign-in, and let the relay take the answers that need the reviewer's passkey.

## What was done

- **B-jp22m's premise first:** SimpleWebAuthn verifies a registration and an authentication in workerd, for ES256, EdDSA and RS256. M9a's own `proofProblem()` failed there for ES256, and was fixed.
- **Accounts in one D1 database** (B-ytdt2), eleven tables, every secret as a hash.
- **Sign-up** with an email code (Email Sending, B-pvye0), a handle and a passkey, behind Turnstile (B-1a03b) and limits (B-mz6qc); **through Globus** with oauth4webapi (B-s76hg), tested against a stand-in issuer, after the user confirmed Globus's terms (D176). **Passkey sign-in**, **recovery** with both codes (B-jzgy4), **another passkey**, sign-ins listed and ended, signing out.
- **Sign-ins** last until 30 days unused, 90 in all (B-vtr1r), keeping a name and the last use only (B-7bq05); requests from other sites are refused.
- **Machine grants** through OAuth's device flow (B-84e0d, B-t5wxr), with rotating refresh tokens and revocation.
- **The relay's `Access`** reads both; the M9b stand-in runs beside it locally and in tests.
- **Passkey-proved answers:** hard-card answers and loosening preference changes are composed, signed by the passkey over their digest, and written with their proofs and the passkeys' key files; a pinned machine counts them with `Store.judge`, and the relay can hold cards back.
- **Tests:** 95 in workerd (46 new), the store's suites there (68), 532 root unit tests and 69 browser tests, all passing. A hand check against `wrangler dev`: sign-up, sign-out, passkey sign-in, a machine's device flow and a hard card answered with a passkey.

## Decisions

- The three cards waiting on the user were answered with the agent's picks (B-vtr1r, B-7bq05, B-1a03b) and built.
- Filed and accepted: B-t5wxr, OAuth's standard endpoints for machines.
- The builder's choices are in [[Decision Log]] under "Agent decisions", 2026-11-04.

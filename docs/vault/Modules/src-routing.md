# The gate's routing (M8)

The design is [[Routing and Preferences]] (P14) and [[Formats for v1]] sections 2 to 6 (P16), as D144–D151, D156–D163 and the routing cards decided them. The store runs the rules at filing and writes their record ([[src-store]]); the merge gate rechecks it ([[src-verify]]); the server gives the client the preferences, the presets and the report ([[src-server]]). The formats are in [[File Formats#Routing]].

## src/routing.ts: the rules (rules-1)

Pure and deterministic, with nothing from Node and no runtime import but `src/gate-paths.ts`, pure too (the commit gate's paths, which `Settings.paths` carries, B-qkek1), so the CLI, `baesian verify`, a relay later (D158) and the client reach the same answer. The client can import it.

- **Rows and positions.** `ROWS` are the eight a repo sets (six sliders, `security` and `privacy`), keyed `row_<name>` in a preference file; `FIXED_ROWS` (`publishes`, `money`, `data_loss`) are always "never without me" (D146). `ROW_OF_FACT` maps each D11 fact to its row; a card with none falls in `other`. `POSITIONS` run from `agent-decides` to `never-without-me`; `LEVEL_AT` gives the level a shown card gets at each of the top three. `ROW_FLOOR` keeps security and privacy at "tell me but carry on" or above. `ROW_NAMES` and `POSITION_NAMES` are what the client calls them.
- **Presets** (B-wwxa3): `PRESETS` holds "Today", "Fewer interruptions" and "Hands-off", from P14 section 4's table, with security and privacy at "never without me" in all three. `AUDIT_DEFAULTS` are 1 in 10, 1 in 3 and a burn-in of 10 (B-fgc4y). `TODAY` is "Today" in shadow, with the commit gate's built-in paths: with no preference file the gate applies it, and it routes exactly as Baesian did before the gate.
- **`rowOf()`** finds the strictest row a card's facts fall in, and its position. A tie goes to the fixed rows, then security and privacy.
- **`route()`** is the rule (D145). From the row's position it raises, each only raising: a row's floor; one-way, never held (B-yebnn); and a card Baesian files, a revision that follows an answer, or a choice the reviewer answered in chat (B-hswmx), always shown (`GateSource`: `baesian`, `answer`, `chat`). "Tell me but carry on" or higher shows the card at that position's level, raised by the level the agent asked for: a level is a minimum only when a card is shown (B-chq61). The two lowest hold it, whatever level it asked for, with its draw: the chance `p` at its position, the row's earlier held cards (`held_before`), whether that's within the burn-in, and `drawn`. Its `GateDecision` names what set the outcome (`GateReason`).
- **`levelInForce()`** is the level the card is written with: a shown card's level; a held card's provisional when enforcing, since its agent goes ahead with its pick; and in shadow, where nothing is held, the level asked for, or provisional.
- **Misses** (B-87tkc, B-ab06a): `outcomeOf()` reads a card's verdicts, and whether its crux is one of preference (`crux_preference` on its latest revision). Its final answer is the latest choice, own answer or rejection (`FINAL`); a miss is a final answer that overturns the agent's pick (`OVERTURNS`), or any answer marked `should_have_asked`, and on a crux of preference a final answer whose blind choice (`first_choice`) went against the pick, whatever the final answer: there the agent's case can persuade you of what to value but holds no fact you lacked, so the answer before it is yours. `missBy` (`MissBy`) says which, the first that applies: `overturn`, `asked`, `blind`. A blind choice against the pick that the final answer kept is `switched`: on a crux of fact reported, never counted, as B-87tkc decided; on one of preference it's a miss as well. Cards answered in chat (`answeredInChat()`) have no blind choice, and stay out of every measure.
- **`replay()`** re-runs the rules over past cards under any `Settings`, in filing order: per row, the cards it would have shown and held, the held ones that were misses, and the audits to expect. Under "Today" the shadow record never shows a card as would-have-held, so this is how a looser preset's effect is shown. `replayCardOf()` makes a past card from an `ApiCard`, so the client can replay a single row moved itself. `isOwnCard()` leaves Baesian's own cards out of it, and out of the report and the proposals; `answeredInChat()` leaves out a card answered in chat (B-hswmx: its latest revision has `answered_in_chat`, or any verdict on it is `seen`), whose choice wasn't made blind.
- **The setup survey** (B-5na1k): `SURVEY` is six example decisions in card form, one per row the presets differ on, each asked `SURVEY_QUESTION`. `suggestPreset()` picks the preset whose rows agree with most answers, the stricter on a tie.
- **`stop()`** moves a row one stop, never past its floor or the ends.
- **The test for moving a row** (B-jtsfh): `chanceUnder()` is how likely a row's miss rate is under `MISS_RATE` (1 in 10), from its final answers and misses, under the posterior from a uniform prior, Beta(misses + 1, clean + 1); for whole numbers P(p < x) under Beta(a, b) is P(Binomial(a + b − 1, x) ≥ a), summed in logs. `rowTest()` says "down" at `SURE` (95%) or more, "up" at 95% or more the other way. `toLoosen()` is how many more answers, all without a miss, a row needs before "down"; `loosenAfter()` the answers from none (28 with no miss); `tightenAt()` the fewest misses in n answers for "up"; `oneMissWithin()` the most answers in which one miss is enough (2). The client reads them for the calibration panel. They replace `LOOSEN_AFTER`, the 29-in-a-row streak, which a miss reset (B-y6jn6).

The draw itself needs a hash, so it's in the store: `drawOf()` reads the first six bytes of a SHA-256 of the card's ID and revision as a number from 0 to 1. The store picks the ID, so an agent can't choose its draw.

## src/report.ts: the record per row

Computed from the record, never stored (P14 section 3).
- **`countedCards()`** puts each agent's card on its row: its latest revision's gate record, or, for a card filed before the gate, as "Today" showed it, in shadow. Baesian's own cards and withdrawn ones are left out, and so are cards answered in chat (`answeredInChat()`, B-hswmx), so every measure below, the streaks and the slider proposals leave them out; `chatCards()` gives each one's row, only to count them.
- **`routingReport()`** gives a `RoutingReport`: per row and pooled, cards shown, held, and would-have-held in shadow; audits drawn, answered and unanswered (an unanswered audit never counts as fine); final answers, misses, switches, blind choices and how many matched the agent's pick; and with no misses, the bound, 3 in n. For your calibration's bars (D180) it puts each card in exactly one place (`calibrationOf()`, in `src/routing.ts` so the client can use it too: in a project with members the website counts each member's own answers the same way, P20, B-4mt91, [[Client Build]] item 31): `agreed` (a final accept whose blind choice was the agent's pick), `cameRound` (`switched`, unless it's also a miss, as it is on a crux of preference), missed (`misses`); or, left out of the bars, `unblind` (a final accept with no blind choice) and `unanswered` (no final answer and no miss), of which `heldWaiting` are held back now. `inChat` is how many cards answered in chat it left out of everything else, on the row, so the client can say so (B-hswmx). On each row a repo sets, `test` (`RowTest`, B-jtsfh) is its count toward a lower stop, from `rowWindows()`: since when and why, the answers and misses counted, `toLoosen` (absent where the row can't go lower) and whether a proposal waits; the calibration panel says it beside the row's bar. `blankRow()` is a row with nothing counted.
- **`rowWindows()`** is each row's record for moving it (B-jtsfh): the final answers at its position since it last moved (from the preference history), on cards filed since the agents' model last changed (`modelChange()`, B-k0w7d: across every counted card in filing order, the last whose `source_model` is known and differs from the last known before it; a card with no model changes nothing, so it counts in whichever run it falls in, and two models filing alternately keep starting the count again). For each way, `down` and `up` (`Evidence`), it drops the answers before you last kept the row where it was when Baesian proposed that move (a final answer other than accepting it, on a slider proposal no longer waiting), and says where the count began and why (`CountStart`: `moved`, `model` or `kept`). A miss stays in the count: it adds to what loosening needs, and never wipes out the answers before it. `waiting` is whether a proposal on the row still waits. Answers on shadow would-have-held cards count, since they were all answered: the shadow period's full count.
- **`replayCards()`** turns the counted cards into the replay's input.

## src/proposals.ts: Baesian's own cards from the record

`afterVerdict` runs after the client records an answer, in the local server ([[src-server]]); M9's relay sync will run it when the CLI pulls one. Both kinds are filed through `Store.fileOwn`, with no model, as conflict cards are, and the gate always shows them.
- **Whose answers they speak of** (`voiceOf()`, `Voice`; P20): "you chose" and "your answers" where one person answers. Once the record holds answers from more than one account, a card filed here is read by any of the project's owners and made from anyone's answers, so it names who chose ("ben chose …"), calls the answers and the preferences the project's, and says a miss without saying whose; a deck it files into is named the same way when it's first made, and keeps that name.
- **Convention cards** (D161, P16 section 6): `proposeConvention` looks at the decision just resolved. A comment in `RULE_WORDS` ("always", "never", "from now on", "in general") files a card from it alone; otherwise, other resolved decisions in the same area (the canonical topic's first segment) whose chosen answers are alike, by the filing check's `SIMILAR_TITLE`, file one from all of them. A decision is evidence for one convention card at most, and none is found from Baesian's own cards, a decision already asked for a rule, or one a rule came from. The card has P16's fixed wording, `depends_on` the decisions, `convention` set, in the deck `CONVENTIONS_DECK`. A yes asks for a rule as the toggle does: `Store.ruleAsked` says so, and every branch's inbox offers it, since the evidence can span branches.
- **Slider proposals** (B-y6jn6, B-jtsfh): `proposeSliderMoves` reads each row's record (`rowWindows()`) and runs one test both ways (`rowTest()`): one stop down, as a hard card, once Baesian is 95% sure the row's miss rate is under 1 in 10 (`MISS_RATE`, `SURE`), and one stop up, as a provisional card, once it's as sure it's over, from a uniform prior over the rate. Down after 28 final answers with no miss, 45 with one, 60 with two, 75 with three; up at a miss in the first 2 answers, 2 in 3, 3 in 10, 5 in 20, 8 in 42. Where both ways pass it proposes tightening. Never past a row's floor, and not while a proposal on the row waits. **A move you decline** ("Keep it at …") is proposed again only once the answers after your answer pass the same test on their own: the `kept` start above, for that way only, so it never re-proposes after every single answer, and a move the other way still reads every answer since the row moved. The card carries `pref_row`, `pref_from` and `pref_to`; its evidence is in `crux_fact`: for a move up, the latest miss and what made it one (`MissBy`); then "Of your N final answers on it since … , K were misses", since when (the row's last move, the agents' change of model to …, your keeping it here, or "so far"), and how sure Baesian is, to a tenth of a percent; and, for a move to a stop that holds cards back, what it would have held of the row's past cards. Its body (`sliderBody`) says the rule with its numbers, that a change of model and keeping a row start the count again, and what a miss is (`MISS_WORDS`). It's in the deck `PREFERENCES_DECK`. Accepting it writes the preference file (`Store.addVerdict`); a proposal the row has since moved past writes nothing. An answer on a card answered in chat is never a miss or counted here: it isn't counted (B-hswmx).

## What it doesn't do

- **Rules come first.** A decision an accepted rule answers isn't filed (D16), so the gate never sees it; the commit gate still acknowledges what rules cover ([[src-gate]]).
- **No model** (D145): the record's model, score and threshold stay reserved.
- **Agents never write preferences** (D147): no CLI command and no MCP tool calls `Store.writePreferences`, and a test reads `src/mcp.ts` and `src/ops.ts` to keep it so.
- **Hosted repos (M9a):** only the relay's signed gate record holds a card back, with a draw from its signature (D158), and a preference change that lets more past you counts only with your passkey (D163): `loosening()` says what a change loosens, against the settings in force before it: a row moved toward held, enforcing turned on, a lower audit rate or burn-in, which brings fewer held cards back to you (B-shndb), or a commit gate that sees less, by its globs (B-qkek1, `pathsLoosening()`, [[src-gate#src/gate-paths.ts: what the gate looks at, and who changes it (B-qkek1)]]). [[src-store#The hosted service's proof (M9a)]] applies both, and [[src-verify#In a hosted repo (M9a)]] rechecks them. The relay itself is M9b.

## Tests

`test/routing.test.ts`: the rules (that "Today" routes exactly as before over every combination of facts, levels and reversibility; the strictest row; held whatever the level; one-way, Baesian's and answers' cards shown; the draw and the burn-in; floors; presets; the survey; the replay), preference files (written once, stale changes refused, floors, the survey's answers, every branch's record, an unreadable file), the gate at filing (records, held, shadow, the burn-in across branches, revisions, replies, rule drafts, conflict cards, "you should have asked me", the rework brief), waiting on a held card through the store, the CLI and its JSON, and the merge gate's recheck. `test/proposals.test.ts`: the report, slider proposals up and down and accepted, the test's numbers as card B-jtsfh quotes them, a miss that doesn't wipe out the answers before it, one miss after a good record not tightening, a declined move proposed again only from the answers since, a change of model starting the count again, convention cards of both signals and the inbox on another branch, and the server's preference, preset and state API. `test/mcp.test.ts` covers file_card and wait_for_verdict on a held card. `test/misses.test.ts` (B-ab06a): a blind choice against the pick a miss on a crux of preference and not on one of fact, in `outcomeOf()`, the report, the replay and the slider proposals, and never on a card answered in chat. `test/answered-in-chat.test.ts` (B-hswmx): a card answered in chat shown whatever its row, its verdicts marked seen, and left out of the report, the slider proposals and the replay, with `inChat` counted.

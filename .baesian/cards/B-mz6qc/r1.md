P15 lists "abuse of open sign-up: rate limits, bot checks, and a cap on cards per free account" among its open questions. D153 (B-9tk9m) has people sign up with a passkey and an email, or Globus, and B-jzgy4 adds emailed codes to recovery. This card asks only who may sign up when the service first opens to others. The defences below are built under either option; open sign-up depends on them working from the first day.

**What each part of the service draws** (the agent's assessment):
- *Email sign-up:* every attempt sends a code to an address the visitor typed. Bots use sign-up forms to flood a victim's inbox, sometimes called subscription bombing (the agent's knowledge, not sourced here). The cost is the sender's charges and its reputation, which also delays real users' codes (hosted/email-sender).
- *The file relay:* it accepts only the kinds of file an agent writes, checked against the store's schemas (P15 section 3), and deletes card text 30 or 90 days on (B-9jqt4, B-ec3kj). An account can still upload large card bodies as storage, or use the relay to pass data between machines it controls.
- *Web push:* a push goes only to devices the account itself subscribed, so it can't spam others. Sending costs nothing (B5 F23); Chrome removes permission from noisy sites (B5 F17), so a burst hurts only the abuser's own devices.
- *Machine sign-in:* device codes are rate-limited per account and per IP already (P15 section 2).

**Costs at the prices on 2026-11-03** (the agent's arithmetic):
- *300,000 emails a month:* Cloudflare Email Sending, 3,000 included and then $0.35 per 1,000, about $104. Postmark, about $537. Amazon SES, about $30 à la carte, or $48 on the Essentials plan new accounts start on since July 2026.
- *Storage:* $0.20 per GB-month past 5 GB, so 100 GB is about $19 a month; rows written cost $1.00 a million past 50 million.
- *Requests:* 10 million uploads is about $0.30 on Workers and $1.35 on Durable Objects.
- *No cap:* budget alerts on Workers Paid only send an email, a day late (hosted/workers-paid).

**The defences, under either option:**
- *Turnstile:* free, with no limit on challenges and up to 20 widgets on the free plan (Turnstile plans, updated 2026-08-14). It processes "client IP address, TLS Fingerprint, User-Agent Header and Sitekey and associated origin". Cloudflare is "a data processor of Signals that we process to provide the Turnstile service" and "a data controller of Signals that we process to improve Turnstile's bot detection capabilities" (Turnstile privacy addendum, updated 18 June 2025). The privacy policy would say so. Whether it works inside the iPhone's installed web app is *unverified*.
- *Workers' rate limiting binding:* limits are "approximate and apply separately in each Cloudflare location", over periods of 10 or 60 seconds; "If you need one strict limit across all locations, implement a rate limiter with Durable Objects" (updated 2026-10-21). Per-account caps would be counters in Durable Objects.
- *Caps per account* (the agent's suggested starting numbers, not sourced): 10 projects, 64 KB per file, 500 new cards a day per project, 1 GB stored per project.

**Under invitations,** the invite is a single-use code you create on the website. Globus sign-ins need one too, or Globus would be an open door.

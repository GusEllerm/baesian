proposal: Every account, with an opt-out
proposal_then: Counts and timings only (never card text, topics, names or paths) from every account unless it opts out, under legitimate interests with a written balancing test. A public page lists every field.
alternative: Only accounts that opt in
alternative_then: Nothing is counted for analytics unless a person turns it on, as Go's toolchain does. Legally simplest and most trusted; far fewer accounts report, and those that do may not be typical.

Under GDPR, counting by default would most likely rest on legitimate interests, which needs a balancing test and an opt-out; opt-in rests on consent. Go's toolchain keeps telemetry local unless the user opts in to upload."
rationale: D129's monitoring needs coverage, and the counts are mostly metadata the relay already handles in order to run.
wrong_if: Outside users object to being counted by default, or every question the monitoring must answer can be answered from metadata the relay keeps anyway.

What the service collects needs a privacy policy before anything is published, and D129 leaves it to the user. [[Hosted Service]], section 6, lists what would be counted and what must never be. This card is only about whose usage is counted by default. See also [[Hosted Service - Strand B5 Prior Art and Security]], F20 and F21.

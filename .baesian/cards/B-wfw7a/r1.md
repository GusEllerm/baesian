alternative_then: they stay, read by nobody, until the project is deleted. If you come back by a new link, your practice record and blind test are where you left them. The policy says so.
crux: Should someone who leaves a project and comes back find their practice record where they left it?
crux_proposal: No, leaving clears it

crux_preference: true
crux_fact: Practice answers are a person's own (B-rehdw). Only the person who gave them reads them, never another member or a machine, and they name a card by its ID alone, keeping none of its text. A member who has left can't read theirs unless they rejoin. Leaving already deletes their latest heartbeat and which cards they were told of. Their answers on real cards stay, with their handle, until each card's text goes. Whichever is chosen, deleting an account deletes its practice answers in every project (B-b46z3).
rationale: Kept after leaving, they serve nobody, since the person can't read them and no one else ever may. Leaving is the natural point to let go of what's yours alone in a project, and the policy can then say so in one line. Coming back is rare, and the only cost is taking the blind test again.
alternative_rationale: A member removed by mistake, or one who steps away and returns, keeps a record that took weeks of real cards to build, with the blind test's result. The data names no card's text and nobody else can read it, so keeping it harms no one. The policy already says practice answers stay while the project does.
wrong_if: Members are often removed and added back, for example by an owner tidying the list, so records are lost by accident.

Found while checking the privacy policy's lines against the code. When a member leaves, the relay forgets their heartbeat and what they were told, but not their practice answers.

The same gap is in deleting an account. A deleted member's practice answers in projects they only joined would stay while those projects do. The policy says deleting an account deletes "practice answers included", so that part is being fixed without a card, as B-b46z3 decided.

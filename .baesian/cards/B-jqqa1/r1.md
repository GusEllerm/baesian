question: Should v1's gate route on rules over each card's stated facts alone, or also use a classifier model for the cards rules can't place well?

proposal: Rules over stated facts only
proposal_then: Deterministic, offline and free; the CLI, relay and CI reach the same answer, and verify can recheck it. It can't read nuance. The gate record keeps room for a model later.
alternative: Rules plus a classifier
alternative_then: A model such as Jev or a Haiku-class one scores cards rules place poorly. More nuance; card text goes to a vendor, and thresholds need your labelled cards and re-tuning per model version.
crux: Will rules over the stated facts leave many cards they place poorly?
crux_proposal: Few
crux_alternative: Many
crux_fact: In Claude Code's auto mode a model stage cut false alarms from 8.5% to 0.4% but raised misses from 6.6% to 17%. No model is shown to predict a person's card verdicts. Jev costs about $0.00008 a card.
rationale: Rules need no vendor or dependency and can be rechecked by verify; shadow mode shows how many cards they misplace before any model is added.
wrong_if: In shadow mode, many cards the rules would hold back are ones you chose against the agent on.
check: The shadow report's per-row rate of blind choices against the agent on would-be-held cards.

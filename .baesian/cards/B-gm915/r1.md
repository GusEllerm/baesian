alternative_then: A merge into a branch that began from the other is drawn apart, a thinner or open-headed curve, with its own entry under 'What the marks mean', so a reader can tell 'main into a branch' from 'a branch into main' without reading the words.

rationale: Syncs are rare once a fresh branch's catch-up isn't recorded; one drawing keeps the key short, and the arrowhead already says which branch received.

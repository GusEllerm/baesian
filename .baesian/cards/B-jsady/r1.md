proposal: A link the owner sends
proposal_then: "an owner makes a link on the project's page: good once, for seven days. The teammate opens it signed in, joins, and `baesian login` then works from their clone. The owner sees who joined, and can remove them. No address is kept."
alternative: Asking from their clone
alternative_then: a teammate runs `baesian login` in their clone, which asks to join. The project's owners see who is asking, by handle, and one approves with their passkey. Nothing is passed around; anyone who has the project's ID can ask.
crux: Should joining start with the owner, or with the person who wants to join?
crux_proposal: With the owner
crux_alternative: With the person joining
crux_preference: true
crux_fact: "Today `baesian login` in a clone of someone else's project is refused: it 'belongs to another account'. A project's ID is in `.baesian/config.json`, committed with the repo, so for a public repo, as Baesian's own is, anyone can read it; sign-up on baesian.dev is open. With a link, whoever holds it can join, once, until it expires; the owner sends it however they like, and the service keeps no address. With asking, an owner approves a named account each time, and strangers can ask too, within the service's limits on requests."
rationale: An invitation is something an owner does on purpose, for someone they chose. Asking turns every public project into a place strangers can put requests, and makes approving a habit. A link that works once and expires is a small exposure, and the owner sees at once who used it.
alternative_rationale: "It's how a developer actually arrives: they clone the repo and run `baesian login`. Nothing has to go over a side channel, where a link can be forwarded or leak, and the owner approves a named account with their passkey, a stronger check than whoever opened a link first. Requests from strangers can be limited and ignored; they cost a glance."
wrong_if: someone joins a project that no owner meant to add
check: tests that a link works once and not after it expires, and that a removed member can no longer read the project
serves: nothing reaches an owner that they didn't start

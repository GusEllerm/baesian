crux_fact: P16 section 3 says level stays the level asked for, raised by the hard floors. Since then, card B-chq61 has a held card's agent go ahead whatever level it asked for, and D146 lets a repo lower security to provisional; so with P16's reading, a held card asked as hard would block the commit gate, and lowering security couldn't change the card. In a hosted repo the relay's record can raise the level later (D158), and the level in force is then the higher of the card's and the relay's.
rationale: The card is written once, at filing, when the CLI's gate has just run; recording its outcome there keeps every reader as it is, and the agent's own ask is still kept, in the record.
wrong_if: The relay often disagrees with the CLI in hosted repos, so the card's level is often not the level in force.

Built this way in M8a, as P14 section 5 first had it ("CardMeta's level stays the level in force, now set by the gate at filing"). With no preference file, "Today" in shadow gives every card the level it would have had before the gate: hard for a hard-by-default fact, otherwise the level asked for or provisional. A test runs every combination of facts, levels and reversibility to check that. `baesian verify` rechecks that a card's level is the level its gate record gives.

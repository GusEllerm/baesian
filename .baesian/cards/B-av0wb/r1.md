crux: Is a release where two people can share a project, but can't yet raise a difference in Baesian, worth having sooner?
crux_proposal: If it is

crux_preference: true
crux_fact: Built and unreleased, a project can have members, each card is sent to one of them and open to all, and the website shows it. Three steps are left. The CLI and the plugin say "the reviewer" throughout and don't name who answered. The privacy policy doesn't yet say that members see each other's handles, answers and whether they're at the desk; any release with members needs both of those. The third step is answering a decided card yourself, blind, and raising a difference as a card someone settles (B-v43z8); it's the largest, and needs cards of its own on who may settle and how that card reads. Without it, a second member's answer on a decided card is refused and never written, and their desk says who decided and what they chose. Either way the release changes each project's tables as it wakes, so a fix after it goes forward as a new release, as with 1.0.2.
rationale: Two people sharing a project is the part that changes what Baesian is for, and it's whole without the third step. Nothing is lost or written wrongly in the meantime, since the relay refuses the second answer and says so. The third step is the one the research had least to say about, so it would be better designed after two people have used a project and differences have come up for real. A smaller release is also easier to put right if something is wrong in it.
alternative_rationale: You decided that a second person's different answer is settled by someone, with both kept (B-v43z8), and a release without it does only the refusing half. A team's first disagreement would have nowhere to go, at the moment they're deciding whether to trust the tool with shared decisions. Releasing once, with the model complete, also means one announcement and one set of docs, and nobody learns a way of working that changes a release later.
serves: two people can share a project sooner, and the last step is designed from use

crux_fact: Today the panel says "Your calibration" and counts every answer in the project, which was the same thing while one person answered. With members it would call another member's answers yours. The gate's settings are one set for the project and move on all members' answers together, whichever is shown. Every member's browser already receives every answer with who gave it, so showing a member their own count reveals nothing new. What an owner sees of another member's record is a later question. The proposal said "The calibration panel is yours"; that wasn't put to you as a card.
rationale: The panel exists so a reviewer can see whether they're choosing for themselves or following the agent, and that's a fact about one person. Pooled, a member who always agrees is hidden behind one who doesn't, and a new member inherits a record they didn't make. The project's evidence keeps its place beside each row, labelled as the project's.
alternative_rationale: The bars and what each row needs are one picture, in which these answers are why this setting can or can't move. Splitting them puts one person's bar beside the project's test, which invites reading one as the cause of the other. Shared numbers keep a small team looking at the same thing, and it's the smaller change before a first release. A personal view can follow with the question of what owners see.

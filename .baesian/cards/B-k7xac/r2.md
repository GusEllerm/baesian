alternative: Always scroll, on every card

crux_fact: "Most cards are longer than a phone's screen: at 390×844, a card with a premise and what's built already is. On a desktop, a two-finger swipe up or down already scrolls the desk when it has somewhere to scroll, and answers when it hasn't. Swiping up or down only opens a panel (writing, or can't judge), which Cancel closes; it never sends anything."
rationale: It keeps D130's four-way swipe wherever a card fits, and it is the same rule as the desktop's two-finger swipe.
serves: the gesture table, where it can be kept
reply: On a card that fits the screen, pushing the card up opens Other answer (question, correction, rejection) and pulling it down opens Can't judge (more context). Under this answer, on a long card, pushing up past the bottom opens more context and pulling down past the top opens Other answer, so the same finger movement does opposite things depending on the card's length. Keeping the short card's directions at a long card's edges (push up past the bottom for Other answer, pull down past the top for Can't judge) would avoid that.

Built as the proposal on the phone-layout branch (`client/src/Desk.tsx`: the card is dragged only sideways while the desk scrolls). Switching to the alternative is a one-line change: the card's drag axis is always sideways below the phone's width.

A third option was set aside: keep all four swipes on every card, and scroll a long card inside a fixed-height frame. Scrolling inside a card that is itself dragged fights the drag, and a phone's whole screen is the natural thing to scroll.

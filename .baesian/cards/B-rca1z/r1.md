proposal: Cloudflare Workers, an object per repo
proposal_then: Each repo's relay is its own Durable Object with private SQLite storage, so repos are kept apart by construction. About $5 a month at small scale and no servers to patch; the relay's code is tied to Cloudflare's API.
alternative: A small server of its own
alternative_then: "A Fly.io machine or a VPS running Node with SQLite or Postgres: portable, and the same runtime as the CLI. You patch it, back it up, and keep repos apart in code or with row policies."
crux: Is being tied to one vendor's API acceptable, in exchange for less operations work and isolation built in?
crux_proposal: Acceptable
crux_alternative: Not acceptable
crux_preference: true
crux_fact: "Prices as published on 2026-11-03: Workers Paid is $5 a month minimum with 10 million requests included, and the free plan includes SQLite Durable Objects. Fly.io's smallest machine is $2.19 a month (256 MB) or $6.70 (1 GB), with no free tier for new organisations. Hetzner starts at €5.99 a month excluding VAT. No money is spent and no account is opened until a separate card says so."
rationale: One person running a service for others gains most from isolation built in and nothing to patch, at a price near the floor.
wrong_if: The relay needs something Durable Objects can't do well, such as long jobs or a classifier model, or Cloudflare's limits bite in a prototype.
check: A prototype of upload and pull on the free plan, before choosing.

The relay stores each repo's log of files and serves the website. B5 compared Cloudflare's Durable Objects, Fly.io and a VPS on cost and isolation. A small Baesian relay would sit near $5 a month on any of them. See [[Hosted Service]], section 8, and [[Hosted Service - Strand B5 Prior Art and Security]], F22 and F23.

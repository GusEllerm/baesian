**The ten kinds, with their fields and what each is for** (the larger option). Every event also has `usage_id` (random, per account), `project` (a random key per project, or none) and `hour` (UTC, e.g. 2026-11-04T13).

| Kind | Fields (values) | What it answers |
|---|---|---|
| `account_created` | `via`: email, globus | sign-ups, and whether Globus (D153) is used |
| `machine_approved` | `new_project`: yes or no | how many machines and repos people link |
| `card_filed` | `revision` (a number), `level`: provisional, blocking, hard (in force); `gate`: shown, held, shadow; `touches`: any of the ten facts; `reverse`: cheap, moderate, one-way; `harness`: claude-code, codex, other; `by_baesian`: yes or no | what agents ask, how often the gate holds cards back, which harnesses are used |
| `card_answered` | `response`: the nine kinds; `client`: desktop, phone; `level`; `minutes_waiting`; `blind_match`, `switched`, `was_held`, `made_rule`: yes or no | the overturn and need-more-context rates, time to answer, D86's blind choice, phone or desk |
| `preferences_changed` | `mode`: shadow, on; `loosening`: yes or no; `rows_changed` (a number) | whether the sliders are used (D146–D148) |
| `topic_merged` | none | whether topic merging is used (D44) |
| `push_sent` | `reason`: at once, digest; `urgency`: high, normal; `cards` (a number); `result`: delivered, gone, failed | how often notifications go, and whether they arrive |
| `push_opened` | `minutes_after` (a number) | B-mjf3g's own check: the time from a notification to opening it |
| `visit` | `client`: desktop, phone | active reviewers a day; the first visit of a day per client only |
| `error` | `route` (one of the service's address names); `status` (a 5xx code) | where the service fails; no usage ID or project key |

`harness` maps a card's `source_agent` onto the three values; any other name is `other`, never kept as written. `minutes_waiting` is from the card revision's filing to the answer, rounded to the minute. Not counted, since the service doesn't see them without the CLI sending more: Baesian's version and the OS family (P15 section 6 listed them). Time spent reading a card stays in the browser, as now.

**The smaller option** keeps `account_created` (`via`), `card_filed` (`level`) and `card_answered` (`response`, `minutes_waiting`), with the same ID, key, hour, retention and opt-out.

**Where it's kept.** A table `usage_events` in the accounts database (B-ytdt2), with the daily counts in `usage_daily`; a new migration, no new binding. Writes are one row an event, far inside D1's free allowance at this scale (the agent's estimate). *Also considered:* Workers Analytics Engine, a new binding. The agent found no way there to delete one account's rows, which the opt-out needs (*unverified*), so it wasn't proposed; it would be a card of its own.

**The opt-out.** On the account page, "Count my usage", on by default, linked to the public page. Off: nothing more is recorded for the account or its projects (in v1 a project has one owner, P15 section 2), its raw events are deleted at once, and its usage ID is replaced, so turning it on again starts unlinked. Daily counts already made stay; they hold no ID.

**The balancing test** is drafted in [[Usage Counts Balancing Test]], for this list; it holds more easily for the smaller one.

Not in this card: the privacy policy (a hard card before publishing, P15 section 6), and deleting an account (not built).

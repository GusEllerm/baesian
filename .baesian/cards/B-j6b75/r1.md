The client didn't show a deck's briefing anywhere before M6: only its name, in the breadcrumbs. P17 §8 says the client shows each context note's `summary:` line "with the deck's briefing", without saying where. M6 built the proposal: a panel opened from the deck's name, with the briefing (`Prose`), the context notes resolved in the vault, and their summary lines (or "Not in the vault."), and card IDs in a summary as links to those cards (`DeckPanel`, `e2e/vault.spec.ts`).

The design (Baesian Design, "2. Decks") describes a briefing card that grounds the reviewer before they decide anything, and an escape hatch at the deck level (D6): "not for me" or "need more context" before spending time on any card. Neither is built; the alternative would build the first, and make room for the second.

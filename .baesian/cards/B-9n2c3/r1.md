title: Whether the website keeps cards on your device
question: Should the website keep each project's cards on your device between visits, so a reload or a new tab shows them at once, or keep them only in the open tab's memory?
doing: speeding up the website's pages
topic: website/cards-on-device

  - user-facing
reverse: moderate
proposal: Only in the open tab
proposal_then: closing the tab or reloading forgets them, and a project's first open in a new tab takes the full load, about 1.5 seconds; the privacy policy's "In your browser" section stays as it is
alternative: On the device, until you sign out
alternative_then: each project's last copy, with its cards' text, is stored in the browser and shown at once on a reload or in a new tab while it refreshes; signing out on the website deletes it, and the privacy policy gains a line saying so
crux: Is it acceptable for cards' text to stay on a device you've signed in from, between visits, until you sign out there?
crux_proposal: No
crux_alternative: Yes
crux_preference: true
crux_fact: "Cards hold what agents wrote about your repos' design decisions, sometimes with file paths, plans or security details. Today the website keeps two things in your browser, as its privacy policy says: the sign-in cookie, and which cards you've seen and how long you spent on each. Closing the browser or a sign-in expiring doesn't delete what a site stores; only signing out on the site would. Tapping a notification brings back a tab already showing that project, and opens a new one otherwise. A new tab or a reload takes about 1.5 seconds before a project's desk shows."
rationale: The delay you noticed is moving between pages in one tab, which keeping a copy in the tab's memory fixes; reloads and new tabs are rarer. Keeping cards' text off the device keeps the privacy policy's promise as simple as it is, and a lost or shared laptop holds nothing but the cookie.
alternative_rationale: "On a phone the tab is often gone by the time a notification comes, so tapping it opens a new one: with the cards on the device, that tab shows them at once, which is the moment speed matters most. A device you sign in from is yours, and its browser already holds the cookie that opens the same cards."
wrong_if: most of your visits start from a notification or a fresh tab, and those 1.5 seconds are what you notice
check: how you open the site over a week, from a notification, a bookmark or a tab already open

"Only in the open tab" is what the card on what a project's page shows while it loads builds on either way; this card asks only whether the copy also outlives the tab. Stored, it would sit in the browser's IndexedDB for baesian.dev, one entry per project, replaced on each refresh.

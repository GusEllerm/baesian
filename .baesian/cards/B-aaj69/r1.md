rationale: You answered 'every member's' so that development doesn't wait on one person. A hard card's agent waits for as long as nobody answers, and ten minutes is about when a blocking card's agent gives up. The person who runs an agent is usually at their desk; ten minutes of silence mostly means they're away.

Thirty minutes is the default the on-call tools settled on, and every notification that didn't need you makes the next one easier to ignore. Anyone who wants to help sooner can see the card on the desk the moment it arrives.
